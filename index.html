Master's Thesis · Aalborg University · 2024

Augmented Reality in Museums

Designed and developed a web-based AR application to attract new museum audiences and improve interaction without disturbing physical exhibitions.

Research: Desk research · Expert interviews · User testing
Design: Figma · Wireframes · Iterative prototyping
Build: HTML · JavaScript · WebXR / A-Frame
Method: SCRUM · Agile · Qualitative research

Why I chose this project

I wanted to explore the relationship between technology, culture and people. My interest in art and culture made museums a natural context for exploring how emerging technology could create new ways of experiencing art without taking away from the physical exhibition.

Bachelor Project · Aalborg University

Agility in the Danish Public Sector

Investigated how agile methodologies are understood and applied within the Danish public sector through a case study of KOMBIT.

Research: Case study · Interviews · Literature research
Focus: Agile development · Public sector · IT
Context: Danish public sector · KOMBIT
Outcome: Five principles for adopting agile methodology

Why I chose this project

I wanted to understand not only how digital technologies are developed, but also the systems, regulations and organisations surrounding them. Semester Project · Aalborg University

Mediating Information Between Refugees & Volunteers

Developed a concept for a mobile application designed to help newly arrived refugees overcome everyday challenges by connecting them with volunteers who speak their language.

Research: Semi-structured interviews · Desk research
Design: Object-oriented analysis · Prototype
Testing: Usability testing · User feedback
Focus: Accessibility · Information · Community

Why I chose this project

This project was particularly meaningful to me because of my family's experience with migration. It also connected with my growing interest in how access to information, digital tools and legal knowledge can affect people's ability to navigate everyday life. I wanted to explore how technology could help bridge some of these gaps.
